Three calls, two ways

A profile page needs three answers that do not depend on each other: the user, their orders and their recommendations. Sequential requests ask for one, wait for it, then ask for the next. Parallel requests ask for all three at once and wait for the last to arrive. Both call the same real Worker, which waits as long as the sliders say before it answers. Predict each total before you press run. New to these terms? Start here.

Sequential costs the sum of the calls; parallel costs the slowest one. The gap grows with every
    call you add. Sequential is still right when a call needs the previous answer, for example the orders of the user you just
    looked up. Then the waterfall is the shape of the data, not a mistake.
